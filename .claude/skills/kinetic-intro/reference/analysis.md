## Why it works

- **Pacing:** a new event roughly every 1.5 s, and the only long hold is the silent one. The
  silence makes the viewer wait for the answer, so it lands harder.
- **Typography:** one heavy rounded geometric sans for Latin and Devanagari (Poppins + Hind
  feel). Hinglish mixes an English noun with Hindi grammar. Two colours do the storytelling:
  navy asks, saffron answers. The oversized hairline "?" acts as an illustration, not as
  punctuation.
- **Energy:** every entrance is a fast `outExpo` with a blurred trail, every scene change
  carries momentum upward, and the background is never static (arcs, glow, sparkle).
- **Brand:** saffron `#E8641C`, deep saffron `#C44E14`, navy `#15213F`, paper `#F5F2ED`,
  with leaf green as a small accent. The logo shows at the start and the end.

The live-action shots in the other clips (8 s each, 1–3 cuts, a Hinglish subtitle in heavy
white type) follow the same 1.5–3 s shot rhythm.
